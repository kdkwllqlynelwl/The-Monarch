What Monarch is

Turning print into braille, and information into opportunity.

Monarch is a braille translation and access tool designed to help people who are blind or have low vision read and understand text more easily. It transforms written content into braille or readable formats, giving users access to educational materials, documents, and everyday information that might otherwise be difficult to reach.

At its heart, Monarch reflects a simple goal: to make literacy, learning, and communication more open and inclusive for everyone.

How it works

A clear process built around access and clarity.

1

Input

Text is captured from print, digital content, or other accessible materials.

2

Translate

Monarch converts the material into braille using consistent rules that preserve meaning and readability.

3

Deliver

The result can be read by a person using braille, tactile output, or other assistive tools.

By simplifying the conversion from print to braille, Monarch helps reduce barriers to education, work, and daily living. It is built around the idea that access is not a luxury—it is a foundation for independence.

For who it is

Built for people who need access, literacy, and independence.

  • Students learning in school or college
  • People who are blind or have low vision
  • Teachers and support staff creating accessible materials
  • Families and communities seeking equitable access

Monarch is especially valuable in classrooms, workplaces, and public settings where clear access to information can change how a person learns, participates, and grows.

What good it brings

Better access leads to better opportunities.

Monarch helps people gain access to information that supports learning, confidence, and independence. It helps remove barriers that often limit participation in education and daily life. In practical terms, it helps people read what they need, when they need it, and with greater ease.
