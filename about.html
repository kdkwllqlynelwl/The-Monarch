About the American Printing House

Creating access, literacy, and opportunity for people who are blind or have low vision.

The American Printing House for the Blind (APH) is a nonprofit organization committed to helping people who are blind or visually impaired live with greater independence through education, technology, and accessible materials.

Mission Access for every learner

Who they are

APH exists to help people see possibility, not barriers.

The American Printing House for the Blind is one of the leading nonprofit organizations focused on creating accessible educational resources for people who are blind or have low vision. The organization develops braille materials, tactile tools, accessible technology, and educational solutions designed to make learning and daily life more equitable.

What they do

They produce the tools, materials, and resources that make access possible.

A

Braille

They create braille books, educational resources, and formats that support learning.

B

Assistive technology

They help develop tools that make text, keyboards, and digital resources more usable.

C

Accessible education

They support students, teachers, and families with practical materials and solutions.

Why they chose to do this

Because access to information is essential to opportunity.

APH chose this mission because literacy and access are foundational to education, independence, and participation in society. When barriers to reading and communication are reduced, people can learn, work, contribute, and lead fuller lives. That belief continues to guide their work today.

The organization believes that everyone deserves access to the information and tools needed to live independently and with dignity.

“Access to information is not a privilege; it is a pathway to independence.”
